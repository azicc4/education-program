Each school year has three budgets. Academics run Monday to Friday, within a yearly budget of the regular school hours for that grade times a multiplier. Saturdays hold outdoor skills, riding and shooting, sailing, handcrafts and home arts. Sundays hold Scripture, the lives of the saints and spiritual reading. The core units of each year fit these budgets; elective units and the elective parts of core units are extra, taken as time and interest allow.
