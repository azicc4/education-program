The teacher's plan is built from the curriculum and each child's progress, so it changes whenever either does. The teacher reads every core text and prepares each unit one to two months ahead of the student, studies each language a year ahead, and prepares art, outdoor and other physical skills at least six months ahead. A unit taken by two children is prepared once.
